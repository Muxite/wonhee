**Inputs:** 29 posts the user saved: 20 videos and 9 photo/carousel posts. Content ids 2–30, grabbed by `wonhee grab` (runs 10–12).
**Method:**
- wonhee's existing analysis: shots, keyframes, whisper, a vision call per keyframe sample.

- I spot-checked the main claims against the source dossiers.

**Where the data is:**
- Raw dossiers and teardowns: `data/inspire/2026-10-17/` (gitignored, since they contain third-party text).
- Brief: `data/inspire/2026-10-17/BRIEF.md`.

**Cost:**
- About $0.003 per post for grab + analysis (runs 11–12).
- About 430k Haiku tokens for 29 teardowns.
- A text-only teardown in wonhee using the current model would be about $0.001 per post, because the vision work is already paid for in analysis.

## 2. What to record: B-roll bank, first shoot list

Capture standard for every clip:
- 9:16, phone on a tripod, 1080p 30 fps.
- Hold still 1 s before and after the action.
- Keep the **top third empty** (text goes there).
- Daylight from a window, or one lamp.
- No music while recording.
- Record ambient sound anyway; it gets muted later.

**You on camera ("mood plates")**, 5–15 s each. These pair with any POV/"how I…" line:

| # | Plate | Pairs with lines like | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | At the desk, typing, focused, laptop light on face | "POV when you're [activity] and remember [trigger]" | c15 |
| 2 | Seated on a bus, train or plane, eyes closed, earbuds in | "Is there anyone [here] who can [approve X] because [weak reason]?" | c23 |
| 3 | Deadpan stare at camera, hand over mouth | "How I [react] as a [persona]" | c17 |
| 4 | Turning from back to camera, then walking out of frame | a long relatable line held over several shots | c19 |
| 5 | Waking up / sitting up in bed | "POV: you're dreaming about [small win] until [anticlimax]" | c26 |
| 6 | Staring at a laptop, slow lean back, hands on head | deadline / merge-conflict / "it works on my machine" | c15 / c23 pattern |
| 7 | Holding the phone to your ear beside an open laptop | open-loop question overlay | c12 |
| 8 | Selfie rant, 30–50 s, one real pet peeve | talking-head (coupled: record face and voice together) | c25 (9.9M) |

**Places and things (no person needed)**, 3–8 s each:

## 3. What this means for what we build

Ranked by how many reference videos need the capability, and how much time it saves you.

| Capability | Needed by | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Text-layer renderer with 3 presets** (outline sans / band / cursive + colour emoji), positioned in the empty third | 14 of 20 videos | The core of every decoupled format. libass can't do colour emoji (watchouts.md). |
| **B-roll bank**: ingest plates, auto-tag (SigLIP2: person, setting, mood, action), detect where text can sit | 12 decoupled/loose videos | Lets the system pair a line with existing footage, so most posts need no new shoot |
| **Line bank + line writer**: `text_template`s from teardowns, filled in your voice (style memory R6), no-copy guard | every text-led format | Text is the product, so this is where creative time goes |
| **Pairing + render**: line × plate → one-graph render, preview, outbox | all | Turns "footage on Tuesday, text on Friday" into one command |
| **Teardown node** (this experiment as code): text-only call over existing analysis, fixed schema, **closed vocabularies** for format / tools / camera | each new inspiration batch | Haiku's free-text `tools_needed` produced 40 different strings for overlapping ideas. Use enums. |
| **Shoot list generator**: aggregate `footage_to_record` across teardowns, dedupe against the bank, output what to film next | weekly | The "what should I record" answer, kept up to date |
| **Onboard translation**: gemma4:e4b via the local Ollama at analysis time; translate caption, on-screen text and transcript into the dossier | 8 of 29 posts | Tested: correct on the Korean caption, 0.1–0.2 s per caption once loaded, $0. Keep it loaded (15 s cold load). gemma3:12b invented names, so don't use it. |
| **Music gate** for whisper | at least 6 videos | Stops lyrics being treated as speech or captions |
| **Photo/carousel download in grab** | 9 of 29 posts | Those were judged blind (low confidence), and your own feed is mostly carousels |

Translation note: transcription errors propagate. In c27, whisper heard 陸風 ("land breeze") as 綠鳳, and every model rendered it "Green Phoenix". Give the translator the on-screen text and caption as context, and treat low-confidence segments as drafts.

## 4. Caveats
- One analyst model (Haiku), one pass. `replicate` scores and "fit" are its judgement, not measurements.
- 9 photo posts were analysed from captions only.
- 29 posts is a small sample of what you liked, not of what performs. Plays and likes come from a single snapshot, with no follower normalisation.
- Long multi-shot videos (c20, 24 shots; c27, 23 shots) had only part of their shots described by the analysis step's vision sampling.
